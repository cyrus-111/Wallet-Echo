# 💸 Wallet Echo

A fullstack expense tracker built to help you actually understand where your money goes — not just log it.

---

## Why I Built This

Honestly, I built this for myself. As a teen, I kept noticing money just... disappearing. Not from big purchases, but from all the small stuff — a snack here, a random buy there. The kind of spending you don't even know and wonder where the money wnet

I wanted something that would make me stop and think — okay, I've spent this much this month on this category, maybe I should cut back. Most budgeting apps feel like they're built for adults with salaries and debts. This is just a clean, simple tracker that tells you the truth about your spending, so you can actually save for things that matter to you. Also It has a lot of visuals like bar chart and pie chart to make you understand you expenses in an easier way.

---

## What It Does

Wallet Echo is a fullstack web app where you can log your income and expenses, categorize them, and get a clear picture of your financial habits over time. No fluff, just clarity.

---

## Features

- **Add & track transactions** — log expenses and income with a title, amount, and category
- **Category breakdown** — see exactly which areas (food, transport, entertainment, etc.) are eating your budget
- **Balance overview** — your current balance updates in real time as you add entries
- **Transaction history** — a running log of everything you've recorded
- **Delete entries** — made a mistake? remove it instantly
- **Persistent data** — your data is saved to a database, so it's there when you come back
- **REST API backend** — frontend and backend are fully separated with a clean API
- **Create Account** - You can create your account, update details like username, email, password and login through different devices(Compatible with phone).
